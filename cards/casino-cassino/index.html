Rules

Object

Capture scoring cards from the table by matching ranks or building combinations. First to the agreed score wins.

Setup

  • Deal 4 cards to each player and 4 cards face up to the table.
  • Dealer deals in batches of 4 until the deck is gone.

How to play

  1. On your turn, play one card from hand.
  2. Capture table cards that match your played card rank, or capture combinations that add up to your card value.
  3. You may build cards into a declared value if you have a card that can later capture that build.
  4. If you cannot or do not want to capture/build, trail a card face up to the table.

Starter scoring

  • Most cards captured: 3 points. Most spades: 1. Big casino / 10 of diamonds: 2. Little casino / 2 of spades: 1. Each ace: 1.
