Rules

Recommended Portable Game Night variant

Use a focused slap set: doubles and sandwiches only. That keeps the game fast without turning every card into an argument.

Object

Win the whole deck through face-card challenges and legal slap opportunities.

Setup

Deal all cards face down as evenly as possible. Players do not look at their cards.

How to play

  1. Players take turns flipping one card face up into the center pile, flipping away from themselves.
  2. If a face card or ace appears, the next player gets chances to answer: jack = 1, queen = 2, king = 3, ace = 4.
  3. If the challenged player reveals another face card or ace, the challenge passes to the next player.
  4. If they fail, the previous face-card player takes the whole pile.
  5. Anyone may slap doubles, such as 8-8, or sandwiches, such as 8-4-8. First legal slap wins the pile.
  6. A false slap costs one card, placed under the center pile.

Safety note

Use a flat-hand slap and keep drinks away from the pile. The game is chaotic enough without a beverage expansion pack.
