Rules

Object

Win the whole deck by being first to slap jacks.

Setup

Deal all cards face down as evenly as possible. Players keep personal stacks face down and do not look at cards.

How to play

  1. Players take turns flipping one card face up to the center pile, flipping away from themselves.
  2. When a jack appears, everyone may slap the pile. First hand on the pile wins all center cards.
  3. A false slap costs one card, placed under the center pile.
  4. If a player runs out of cards, they may stay in until the next jack; if they miss it, they are out.

Safety note

Use flat-hand slaps and keep drinks away from the pile. This is the friendly version, not a knuckle demolition derby.
