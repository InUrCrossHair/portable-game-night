Rules

Recommended Portable Game Night variant

Use Mao as a secret-rule party game, but keep penalties light. The dealer/rule keeper knows the starting rules and gives penalty cards when players break them. New players should be told only the object and turn structure.

Object

Get rid of all your cards while discovering the hidden table rules through play.

Setup

  • Deal 5 cards to each player. Place the rest as a draw pile and flip one discard.
  • Choose one rule keeper. They may answer rule questions only by saying “That is not the way Mao works” and giving a penalty card when needed.

Public rules to explain

  • On your turn, play one card matching rank or suit, or draw one card if you cannot play.
  • Say “Mao” when you have one card left.
  • Do not discuss the hidden rules during the hand unless your table allows a learning pause.

Starter hidden rules

  • Aces reverse direction. Eights skip the next player. Jacks change suit.
  • Playing a spade requires saying the card name aloud.
  • Failing to say Mao with one card left earns one penalty card.

Party note

Mao is funniest when penalties are gentle and the rule keeper is fair. If the table gets frustrated, reveal the starter rules after the hand and play again.
