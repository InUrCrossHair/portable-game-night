Rules

Recommended Portable Game Night variant

Use standard 4-player Hearts as the base version: pass left, right, across, then hold; hearts are 1 point; queen of spades is 13 points; game ends at 100.

Object

Avoid taking penalty cards. Lowest score wins.

Setup

Deal all cards to 4 players. Each heart is worth 1 point. The queen of spades is worth 13 points.

How to play

  1. Pass 3 cards according to the cycle: left, right, across, then no pass.
  2. The player with the 2 of clubs leads the first trick.
  3. Players must follow suit if able. Highest card of the led suit wins the trick.
  4. Hearts cannot be led until hearts are broken, unless a player has only hearts.
  5. Add penalty points after each hand. Game ends when someone reaches 100; lowest score wins.

Shooting the moon

If one player takes all hearts and the queen of spades, they score 0 and every other player scores 26.
