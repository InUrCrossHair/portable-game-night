Rules

Object

Avoid ending the round with the lowest card.

Setup

  • Give each player 3 lives/tokens. Deal one card face down to each player.
  • Aces are low, kings are high.

How to play

  1. Starting left of dealer, each player looks at their card and may keep it or swap with the player on their left.
  2. A player holding a king may reveal it to block the swap.
  3. Dealer may keep their card or cut the deck to replace it.
  4. Everyone reveals. Lowest card loses one life. Tied low cards all lose a life.

Win

Last player with lives wins.
