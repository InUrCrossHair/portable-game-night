Rules

Object

Score points by making melds of matching ranks, especially canastas: melds of 7 or more cards.

Setup

Use two decks plus jokers. For four players, play partners. Deal 11 cards each.

How to play

  1. Draw two cards, or take the discard pile if you can legally use the top card.
  2. Build melds of 3 or more cards of the same rank.
  3. Discard one card.
  4. A canasta is 7 or more cards in one meld.
  5. To go out, your side usually needs at least one canasta.

Notes

Grouped beside Rummy and Gin Rummy because it is part of the same meld-building family.

First-time player clarifications

  • Canasta is heavier than most games in this kit. Treat this page as a table-friendly overview unless everyone already knows the basics.
  • A meld is 3 or more cards of the same rank. A canasta is a meld of 7 or more cards.
  • Wild cards usually include 2s and jokers. Red and black 3s have special scoring rules.
  • Before play, agree on initial meld requirements, discard-pile pickup rules, and whether a canasta must be clean to go out.
  • Beginner recommendation: keep the printable/reference open while learning rather than trying to memorize all scoring.
