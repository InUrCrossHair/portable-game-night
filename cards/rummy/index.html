Rules

Recommended Portable Game Night variant

Use basic Rummy: draw one, optionally lay down melds, discard one. Allow laying off on existing melds after a player has made their first meld.

Object

Make sets and runs, then go out before the other players.

Setup

Deal 10 cards each for 2 players, 7 cards each for 3–4 players, or 6 cards each for 5–6 players. Place the rest as a draw pile and flip one card to start the discard pile.

Melds

  • Set: 3 or 4 cards of the same rank.
  • Run: 3 or more cards in sequence of the same suit.

How to play

  1. Draw one card from the deck or discard pile.
  2. Lay down a new meld if you can, or lay off cards onto existing melds after your first meld.
  3. Discard one card.
  4. Go out when all your cards are melded or laid off except the final discard.

Scoring

When a player goes out, everyone else scores the cards left in hand: aces = 1, number cards = face value, face cards = 10. Lowest score after a set number of hands wins.
