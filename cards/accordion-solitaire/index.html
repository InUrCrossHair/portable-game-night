Rules

Object

Compress the entire deck into as few piles as possible, ideally one pile.

Setup

Deal cards face up in a row from left to right. You may deal all cards first or reveal them one at a time.

How to play

  1. A pile may move onto the pile immediately to its left if the top cards match rank or suit.
  2. A pile may also move onto the pile three spaces to its left if the top cards match rank or suit.
  3. After moving a pile, close the gap and keep checking for new moves.

Win

A perfect win leaves one pile. For casual play, try to beat your lowest pile count.
