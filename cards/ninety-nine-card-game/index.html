Rules

Object

Avoid being the player who makes the running total go over 99.

Setup

  • Give each player 3 lives/tokens. Deal 3 cards to each player.
  • Place the rest as a draw pile. Start the running total at 0.

How to play

  1. On your turn, play one card, announce the new total, then draw back to 3 cards.
  2. If your play makes the total exceed 99, you lose one life and the round resets.

Recommended card effects

  • Aces add 1 or 11. Number cards add face value. Jacks and queens add 10.
  • Kings keep the total unchanged. Fours reverse direction. Tens subtract 10. Nines set the total to exactly 99.

Win

Last player with lives wins, or play until someone loses all lives and start a new round.
