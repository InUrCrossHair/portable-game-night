Rules

Object

Reveal all cards before the fourth king appears.

Setup

  • Deal 13 piles of 4 cards face down: 12 piles in a clock shape and one center pile.
  • Clock positions match ace through queen. The center pile is kings.

How to play

  1. Start by revealing the top card of the center pile.
  2. Move that card face up to its matching pile: ace to 1, 2 to 2, and so on; queens to 12, kings to the center.
  3. Then reveal the next face-down card from that destination pile and continue.

Win

Win if every card is revealed before the fourth king sends you to an empty center pile.

First-time player clarifications

  • Cards are arranged like a clock: aces at 1, twos at 2, and so on; kings go in the center.
  • Reveal a card and place it under the pile matching its rank, then reveal the next card from that pile.
  • The fourth king usually ends the game because it sends you back to the center with no unrevealed center card. Win by revealing all other cards before that happens.
