Rules

Object

Race to empty your Nerts pile by playing cards into shared ace-to-king foundations.

Setup

  • Each player/team uses a different-backed deck.
  • Deal a 13-card Nerts pile with the top card face up. Deal 4 work piles face up. Hold the rest as your stock.

How to play

  1. Everyone plays at the same time. Build shared center piles from ace to king by suit.
  2. Build your own work piles downward by alternating color.
  3. Flip stock cards three at a time to look for playable cards.
  4. When your Nerts pile is empty, call “Nerts!” and the round stops.

Scoring

Score 1 point for each of your cards in the center. Subtract 2 points for each card left in your Nerts pile. First to an agreed total wins.

Party note

For a crowded table, play in teams of two: one player manages the work piles, the other flips stock and watches the center.

First-time player clarifications

  • Each player needs their own deck with a distinct back design.
  • You may play from your Nerts pile, work piles, and stock/waste to shared center foundations.
  • When someone empties their Nerts pile and calls “Nerts,” the round stops immediately.
  • Sort center cards by deck backs before scoring so each player gets their own cards back.
