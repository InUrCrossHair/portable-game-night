Rules

Object

Be first to play all cards from your hand and stock pile.

Setup

Each player has a 5-card hand and 15-card stock pile. Two center piles start face down, with two 5-card side piles.

How to play

  1. Flip the two center cards.
  2. Both players play at the same time onto either center pile if their card is one rank higher or lower.
  3. Refill your hand to 5 from your stock pile.
  4. If both players are stuck, flip side-pile cards onto the center piles.

First-time player clarifications

  • Two players split the deck. Each player gets a 5-card hand and a personal draw pile.
  • Set two face-up center cards between players. Put small face-down side piles beside them to restart play when both players are stuck.
  • Players play at the same time, placing cards one rank higher or lower than either center card. Suits do not matter.
  • Whenever you play a card, draw back up to 5 from your personal pile if possible.
  • First player to empty both their hand and draw pile wins.
