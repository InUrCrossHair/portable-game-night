Rules

Object

Win tricks with your partner after choosing trump. First team to 10 points wins.

Setup

  • Use 9, 10, J, Q, K, A in each suit. Partners sit across.
  • Deal 5 cards to each player. Turn one card face up as the potential trump suit.

How to play

  1. Starting left of dealer, players may order up the face-up suit as trump or pass. If ordered up, dealer picks up the card and discards one.
  2. If everyone passes, each player may name another suit as trump or pass.
  3. Left of dealer leads. Follow suit if able. Highest trump wins, otherwise highest card of led suit wins.
  4. Right bower is the jack of trump. Left bower is the other jack of the same color and counts as trump.

Scoring

  • Makers score 1 point for 3 or 4 tricks, 2 points for all 5.
  • Defenders score 2 points if they euchre the makers.
