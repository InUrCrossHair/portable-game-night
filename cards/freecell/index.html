Rules

Object

Move all cards to the four foundations, ace to king by suit.

Setup

  • Deal all 52 cards face up into 8 tableau columns. First 4 columns get 7 cards, last 4 get 6 cards.
  • Leave space for 4 free cells and 4 foundations.

How to play

  1. Build tableau columns downward by alternating color.
  2. Move one card at a time, using free cells as temporary holding spaces.
  3. Empty tableau columns can receive any card.
  4. The number of cards you can move as a packed sequence depends on open free cells and empty columns.

Win

Win by moving every card to the foundations.
