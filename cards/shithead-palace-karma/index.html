Rules

Name note

This game is commonly called Shithead. Closely related versions are often called Palace or Karma. Rules vary heavily, so this should become your house-rule version.

Object

Avoid being the last player stuck with cards.

Setup

Deal each player 3 face-down table cards, 3 face-up cards on top of them, and a 3-card hand.

How to play

  1. Play cards equal to or higher than the top discard.
  2. Draw back up to 3 while the draw pile exists.
  3. If you cannot play, pick up the discard pile.
  4. After your hand is gone, play face-up table cards, then face-down table cards blindly.
  5. Last player with cards loses.

Starter special cards

A clean starter set: 2 resets the pile, 10 burns the pile, and 7 forces the next card to be 7 or lower.

First-time player clarifications

  • After the deal, allow players to swap cards between their hand and face-up table cards before play starts.
  • On your turn, play equal or higher than the current top card unless a special card changes the rule.
  • Beginner specials: 2 resets the pile, 10 burns/clears the pile, and 7 means the next play must be 7 or lower.
  • If you play a face-down table card blindly and it is illegal, pick up the discard pile.
  • First player with no hand, no face-up cards, and no face-down cards wins.
