Rules

Portable betting note

Use candy, coins, chips, or any harmless tokens. Set a small token limit and keep it casual.

Object

Win tokens by betting that a third card will rank between two dealt cards.

Setup

  • Each player antes one token into the pot.
  • Aces are high by default. Decide before playing whether aces can also be low.

How to play

  1. Deal two cards face up to the active player.
  2. The player may pass or bet up to the pot that the next card will fall strictly between the two ranks.
  3. Deal the third card. If it is between, the player wins their bet from the pot. If not, they pay their bet into the pot.
  4. If the first two cards are a pair, the player may pass or bet that the third card will be higher or lower.

House rule

If the third card matches either outside card, the player loses double their bet. Skip this rule for a gentler version.

First-time player clarifications

  • Players ante tokens into a pot, then take turns revealing two outside cards.
  • The active player bets whether the next card will fall between them.
  • If the outside cards are equal, use a house rule before play: either redeal them or require the third card to be higher/lower by declaration.
  • If the pot is empty or too small to pay, everyone antes again before continuing.
