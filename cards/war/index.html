Rules

Object

Win all the cards, or have the most cards when you stop.

Setup

Shuffle one deck and deal all cards evenly between two players. Players keep their stacks face down.

How to play

  1. Each player flips the top card of their stack.
  2. Higher card wins both cards and places them at the bottom of their stack.
  3. If there is a tie, each player places three cards face down and one card face up. The higher face-up card wins the whole pile.
  4. Keep playing until one player has all the cards, or set a time limit and count cards when time runs out.

First-time player clarifications

  • Aces are high. Suits do not matter.
  • If there is a war and a player does not have enough cards for the full face-down/face-up sequence, they put in as many as they can. If they cannot reveal a final battle card, they lose the war.
  • For a shorter game, play 10 minutes and count cards; most cards wins.
