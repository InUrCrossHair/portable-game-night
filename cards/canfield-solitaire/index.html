Rules

Object

Build all foundations upward by suit, wrapping king to ace when needed.

Setup

  • Deal 13 cards face down as the reserve, then turn the top reserve card face up.
  • Deal one card to the first foundation to set the starting rank. Deal 4 tableau cards face up.
  • The rest forms the stock.

How to play

  1. Build tableau downward by alternating color, wrapping ace after king if needed.
  2. Build foundations upward by suit from the starting rank, wrapping after king to ace.
  3. Fill empty tableau spaces immediately from the reserve; if reserve is empty, use the waste.
  4. Turn stock cards three at a time to the waste.

Win

Win by moving all cards to the foundations.

First-time player clarifications

  • The reserve pile is the heart of the game; try to empty it.
  • Foundations build upward by suit and may wrap from king to ace if your starting foundation rank requires it.
  • Beginner rule: when the stock is exhausted, turn the waste over and continue; allow unlimited redeals while learning.
