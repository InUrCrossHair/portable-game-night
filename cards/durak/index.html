Rules

Recommended Portable Game Night variant

Use a 36-card deck: 6 through ace in every suit. Lowest trump starts. No passing variant at first.

Object

Avoid being the last player with cards. There is usually one loser, the durak.

Setup

  • Deal 6 cards to each player. Flip one card to set trump, then place the draw pile crosswise on it.
  • The player with the lowest trump attacks first.

How to play

  1. Attacker plays one card. Defender must beat it with a higher card of the same suit or any trump.
  2. Other attack cards must match a rank already on the table.
  3. If defender beats every attacking card, all table cards are discarded and defender attacks next.
  4. If defender cannot or chooses not to defend, they pick up all table cards and the next player attacks.
  5. After a battle, players refill to 6 cards from the draw pile, attacker first.

End

When the draw pile is gone, keep playing until all but one player are out. Last player holding cards loses.
