Rules

Object

Be the first to play all cards from your hand into the shared layout.

Setup

  • Deal 7 cards to each player. Place the draw pile in the center.
  • Flip 4 cards around the draw pile in a cross. The four diagonal corners are reserved for kings.

How to play

  1. Draw one card at the start of your turn.
  2. Play cards onto layout piles in descending order and alternating colors.
  3. Only kings may start corner piles.
  4. You may move an entire pile onto another legal pile if the bottom card fits.
  5. When you cannot or choose not to play more, your turn ends.

Win

First player with no cards in hand wins.

First-time player clarifications

  • Build piles downward in alternating colors, like red 9 on black 10.
  • Only kings may start an empty corner pile.
  • When moving a pile, the bottom card of the moved pile must legally fit on the top card of the destination pile.
  • First player to empty their hand wins the round.
