Rules

Object

Knock down as many card “pins” as possible over 10 bowling frames.

Setup

  • Use ace through 9 only: remove 10s, face cards, and jokers.
  • Deal 10 pin cards face up in a bowling triangle: 4 back, then 3, then 2, then 1 front.
  • Deal 3 ball cards face up.

How to play

  1. Use ball cards to remove exposed pin cards that match the ball value or combine to equal the ball value.
  2. Only pins with no pins in front of them are exposed.
  3. Use up to 2 balls for a normal frame. Clear all pins with the first ball for a strike; clear remaining pins with the second for a spare.

Win / score

Score like bowling, or simply total pins knocked down over 10 frames for a quick solo challenge.
