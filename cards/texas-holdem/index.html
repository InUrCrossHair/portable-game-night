Rules

Portable betting note

Use candy, coins, chips, or tokens. Keep stakes casual and agree on simple limits before dealing.

Object

Make the best 5-card poker hand using any combination of your 2 hole cards and the 5 community cards.

Setup

  • Dealer button rotates each hand. Small blind and big blind post forced bets.
  • Deal 2 cards face down to each player.

How to play

  1. Betting round before the flop.
  2. Flop: deal 3 community cards face up, then bet.
  3. Turn: deal 1 community card, then bet.
  4. River: deal 1 final community card, then bet.
  5. At showdown, best 5-card poker hand wins the pot.

Recommended limit

Use small fixed bets or a no-raise beginner version until everyone is comfortable.
