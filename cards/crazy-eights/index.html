Rules

Object

Be the first player to get rid of all your cards.

Setup

Deal 5 cards to each player. Place the rest face down as a draw pile and flip one card face up.

How to play

  1. Play one card matching the discard by rank or suit.
  2. Eights are wild. When you play one, name the next suit.
  3. If you cannot play, draw one card.
  4. First player out wins the hand.
