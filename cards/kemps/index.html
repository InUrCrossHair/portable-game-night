Rules

Object

Signal your partner when you have four of a kind without the other team catching you.

Setup

Partners sit across from each other and secretly agree on a signal. Deal 4 cards each and put 4 cards face up in the center.

How to play

  1. Swap cards from your hand with center cards.
  2. When no one wants the center cards, clear and reveal 4 new cards.
  3. When you have four of a kind, give the signal.
  4. Your partner calls “Kemps.” Another team may call “Counter-Kemps.”
