Rules

Object

Get as close to 31 as possible in one suit, or avoid having the lowest hand when someone knocks.

Setup

  • Deal 3 cards to each player and 3 lives/tokens each if playing elimination.
  • Place the rest as a draw pile and flip one discard.

How to play

  1. On your turn, draw from the deck or discard pile, then discard one.
  2. Card values: aces 11, face cards 10, number cards face value. Only cards of the same suit add together.
  3. Instead of drawing, you may knock. Everyone else gets one final turn.
  4. Lowest hand loses a life. A hand of exactly 31 is revealed immediately and everyone else loses a life.

Win

Last player with lives wins, or play a set number of rounds and track wins.

First-time player clarifications

  • Your hand value is the best total in one suit only. Cards from other suits do not add to that suit total.
  • If a player knocks, everyone else gets one final turn, then hands are compared.
  • If the knocker ties for lowest or has the lowest hand, use the penalty your group chose for a failed knock.
  • Beginner rule: a natural 31 ends the round immediately.
