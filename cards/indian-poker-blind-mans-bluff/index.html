Rules

Object

Win with the highest card, or convince everyone else to fold, while you cannot see your own card.

Setup

  • Each player antes one token. Deal one card face down to each player.
  • Without looking, each player holds their card on their forehead so everyone else can see it.

How to play

  1. Players bet, call, raise, or fold based on everyone else’s cards and table talk.
  2. After betting, remaining players reveal. Highest card wins the pot.
  3. Aces are high. Ties split the pot or carry it to the next round.

Why it works

It is poker reduced to bluffing and laughter. Good for groups that do not want a serious poker night.
