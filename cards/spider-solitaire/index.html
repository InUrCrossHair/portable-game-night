Rules

Object

Build full descending same-suit sequences from king to ace and remove them from the table.

Setup

Use two decks. Deal 10 columns: first 4 columns get 6 cards each, remaining 6 get 5 cards each. Only top cards are face up.

How to play

  1. Move face-up cards in descending order.
  2. Move a packed sequence together only when it is the same suit.
  3. Empty columns can receive any card or valid packed sequence.
  4. When stuck, deal one stock card onto each column.
  5. Remove complete king-to-ace same-suit sequences. Win by removing all 8.

Easier variants

Use one suit for easiest play, two suits for medium, or four suits for the full challenge.

First-time player clarifications

  • For a first game, use one-suit Spider if possible. Two-suit and four-suit are much harder.
  • Build tableau cards downward by rank. Full King-to-Ace sequences of the same suit are removed.
  • Deal a new row from the stock only when every tableau column has at least one card.
