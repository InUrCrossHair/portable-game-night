Rules

Object

Collect four of a kind, then grab a spoon before they are gone.

Setup

Put one fewer spoon than players in the center. Deal 4 cards each.

How to play

  1. Draw and pass one card left continuously.
  2. When you have four of a kind, grab a spoon.
  3. Once one spoon is taken, anyone may grab one.
  4. The player without a spoon gets a letter or is eliminated.
