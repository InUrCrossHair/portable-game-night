Rules

Object

Get rid of all your cards by playing cards face down and bluffing when needed.

Setup

Deal all cards evenly. Play proceeds by rank: aces, twos, threes, and so on.

How to play

  1. Place 1 or more cards face down and announce the required rank.
  2. Any player may call “BS” or “Cheat.”
  3. Reveal the cards. If the player lied, they take the pile. If they told the truth, the caller takes the pile.
  4. First player with no cards wins.
