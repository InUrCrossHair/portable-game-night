Rules

Recommended Portable Game Night variant

Use standard 2-player cribbage to 121 points. A cribbage board is nice, but paper scoring works fine for a travel kit.

Object

Score points from card combinations during play and from your hand/crib. First to 121 wins.

Setup

  • Deal 6 cards to each player. Each player discards 2 cards to the dealer’s crib.
  • Cut one starter card after discards. If the starter is a jack, dealer scores 2 for heels.

How to play

  1. Players alternate playing cards without letting the running total exceed 31.
  2. Score during play for 15s, 31s, pairs, runs, and last card.
  3. After play, count non-dealer hand, dealer hand, then dealer crib using the starter card.
  4. Deal alternates each hand. First player to 121 wins.

Counting reminders

  • Fifteens score 2. Pairs score 2. Runs score their length. Reaching 31 scores 2. Last card scores 1. A jack matching the starter suit scores 1 for nobs.
