Rules

Object

Build melds and reduce deadwood, then knock or go gin.

Setup

Deal 10 cards to each player. Flip one discard.

How to play

  1. Draw from the deck or discard pile.
  2. Form sets and runs. Unmatched cards are deadwood.
  3. Discard one card.
  4. Knock when deadwood is 10 points or less, or go gin when all cards are melded.

Scoring starter

Aces = 1, face cards = 10, number cards = face value.
