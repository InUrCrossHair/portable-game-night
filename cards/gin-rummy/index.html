Rules

Object

Build melds and reduce deadwood, then knock or go gin.

Setup

Deal 10 cards to each player. Flip one discard.

How to play

  1. Draw from the deck or discard pile.
  2. Form sets and runs. Unmatched cards are deadwood.
  3. Discard one card.
  4. Knock when deadwood is 10 points or less, or go gin when all cards are melded.

Scoring starter

Aces = 1, face cards = 10, number cards = face value.

First-time player clarifications

  • Deadwood is the value of cards not used in melds. Number cards count face value; face cards count 10; aces count 1.
  • You may knock when your deadwood is 10 or less. Lay down melds, then the opponent may lay off matching cards if allowed.
  • The knocker scores the difference in deadwood. If the opponent has equal or lower deadwood, the opponent undercuts and scores a bonus.
  • Going gin means all cards are in melds except the discard. Gin scores a bonus. Play to 100 points.
