Rules

Recommended Portable Game Night variant

Use standard 4-player partnership Spades: spades are always trump, teams bid total tricks, and bags matter. Keep nil bids optional for the first few games.

Object

Bid how many tricks your team will take, then try to hit that bid.

Setup

Deal all cards to four players in partnerships. Each player bids a number of tricks. Partner bids are combined into a team bid.

How to play

  1. The player left of the dealer leads any non-spade card to the first trick.
  2. Players must follow suit if able. If unable, they may play a spade or discard.
  3. Highest card of the led suit wins unless a spade is played; highest spade wins.
  4. Spades cannot be led until broken, unless a player has only spades.
  5. Score after 13 tricks.

Scoring

If your team makes its bid, score 10 points per bid trick plus 1 point per extra trick. Extra tricks are bags. At 10 bags, subtract 100 points and clear the bag count. If your team misses its bid, subtract 10 points per bid trick.

Optional nil

A nil bid means that player tries to take zero tricks. Successful nil scores +100; failed nil scores -100. Keep this optional until the table is comfortable.
