Rules

Object

Remove the whole pyramid by pairing exposed cards that total 13.

Setup

  • Deal 28 cards into a pyramid of 7 rows, overlapping so only uncovered cards are available.
  • Place the rest as a stock pile.

How to play

  1. Remove pairs of exposed cards totaling 13. Kings are worth 13 and remove alone.
  2. Values: ace 1, jack 11, queen 12, king 13.
  3. Turn stock cards one at a time to the waste and pair them with exposed pyramid cards or the top waste card if your house rules allow.

Win

Win by clearing the pyramid. For an easier version, allow cycling through the stock more than once.
