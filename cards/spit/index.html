Rules

Object

Clear your tableau faster than your opponent.

Setup

Split the deck evenly. Each player builds five tableau piles of 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5 cards, with top cards face up.

How to play

  1. Both players flip one spit card into the center.
  2. Play tableau cards onto either center pile one rank higher or lower.
  3. Fill empty tableau spaces with face-up cards.
  4. When stuck, both players spit new center cards.
  5. When one player clears their tableau, slap the smaller center pile.
