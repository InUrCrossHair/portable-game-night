Rules

Recommended Portable Game Night variant

Use a clean casual version: 3 is low, ace is high, 2 is the highest rank, and legal plays are singles, pairs, or triples. Skip complicated bombs/revolutions for the base rules.

Object

Get rid of your cards first and climb the ranks for the next hand.

Setup

Deal all cards as evenly as possible. The player with the 3 of clubs leads the first hand. After the first hand, the lowest-ranked player leads.

How to play

  1. Lead a single, pair, or triple.
  2. Each next player must play the same number of cards at a higher rank, or pass.
  3. Players who pass are out until the pile clears.
  4. When everyone passes, the last player who played clears the pile and leads next.
  5. First player out becomes President next hand. Last player out gets the lowest rank.

Rank privilege

For the next hand, the lowest-ranked player gives their best card to the President, and the President gives back any one card. For larger groups, the second-highest and second-lowest players may also trade one card.
