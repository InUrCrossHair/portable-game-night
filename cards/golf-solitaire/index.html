Rules

Object

Clear the tableau by playing cards one rank higher or lower than the waste card.

Setup

  • Deal 7 columns of 5 cards face up.
  • Place the remaining cards as a stock, then flip one card to start the waste.

How to play

  1. Move any exposed tableau card to the waste if it is one rank higher or lower than the current waste card.
  2. Suits do not matter. In the starter version, aces and kings do not wrap.
  3. When no tableau card can play, flip one stock card to the waste.

Win

Win by clearing all tableau cards before the stock runs out.

First-time player clarifications

  • Play cards from the tableau onto the waste if they are one rank higher or lower than the waste card.
  • Suits do not matter.
  • Beginner rule: no stock recycling. When the stock is gone and no moves remain, count remaining tableau cards as your score; lower is better.
