Rules

Object

Score points by marking numbers on rows. The more marks you make in a row, the more that row is worth.

Setup

Official Qwixx uses colored dice and printed score sheets. For a homemade version, assign four dice as colors and use a paper score sheet with rows of numbers.

A simple sheet can have rows like:

  • Red: 2 through 12
  • Yellow: 2 through 12
  • Green: 12 through 2
  • Blue: 12 through 2

How a turn works

  1. The active player rolls all dice.
  2. Every player may use the two neutral dice to mark a number.
  3. The active player may also combine one neutral die with one assigned/color die to mark a number in that row.
  4. Numbers usually must be marked from left to right, so skipped numbers are locked out.
  5. If the active player cannot or does not mark anything, they usually take a penalty.

Why it works

Unlike many dice games, players stay involved even when it is not their turn. It has quick decisions, light risk, and enough scoring tension to replay often.

First-time player clarifications

  • Use 2 neutral dice and 4 “row dice.” If your dice are all the same color, assign four dice to rows before the game starts and mark them on the sheet.
  • Each turn, the active player rolls all 6 dice. Everyone may mark the neutral-dice total in any row where that number is still legal.
  • The active player may also mark one row using one neutral die plus that row’s assigned die.
  • Numbers must be marked left to right. Once you skip a number in a row, you can never mark that number later.
  • To lock a row, you usually need at least 5 marks in that row and then mark the far-right lock number. A locked row cannot be used again.
  • If the active player marks nothing, they take one penalty. End the game after 4 penalties by one player or after 2 rows are locked. Score rows by number of marks; most points wins.
