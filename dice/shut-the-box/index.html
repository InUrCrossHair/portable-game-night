Rules draft

Object

Close as many numbers as possible. If playing with multiple people, the player with the lowest remaining total wins.

Setup

Write numbers 1 through 9:


1 2 3 4 5 6 7 8 9

How a turn works

  1. Roll two dice.
  2. Add the dice together.
  3. Cross off one or more open numbers that add exactly to the roll.
  4. Keep rolling until you cannot make the rolled total using open numbers.
  5. Add the numbers left open. That is your score.

Example

You roll an 8. You could close:

  • 8
  • 7 + 1
  • 6 + 2
  • 5 + 3
  • 4 + 3 + 1

Only open numbers can be used.

Why it works

Shut the Box is quick, clean, and easy to replay. It feels like a small math puzzle with enough luck to keep everyone in it.
