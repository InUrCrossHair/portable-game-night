Rules

Variant status: Ready. Uses a simplified non-gambling version with a beginner betting mat.

Object

Have the most chips after a set number of rounds.

Setup

Each player starts with 20 fake chips.

Game length

  • Short game: 10 rounds
  • Normal game: 20 rounds
  • Long game: play until someone reaches 40 chips or loses all chips

How a round works

  1. Players place fake-chip bets on the betting mat.
  2. Roll 3 dice.
  3. Resolve winning and losing bets.
  4. Continue until the agreed number of rounds is complete.

Beginner bets to include

Big

Total is 11–17, except triples.

Payout: 1:1

Small

Total is 4–10, except triples.

Payout: 1:1

Odd / Even

Total is odd or even.

Payout: 1:1

Specific number appears

Choose a number 1–6.

  • Appears once = 1:1
  • Appears twice = 2:1
  • Appears three times = 3:1

Exact total

Bet on the exact total from 4–17.

Payout: 5:1

Any triple

Any three matching dice.

Payout: 10:1

What to exclude

Do not include every real Sic Bo bet. Skip specific triples, two-dice combinations, domino-style combinations, and complex payout tables.

Why it works

Sic Bo gives a casino-night option besides Craps. This version keeps the casino-table flavor but stays friendly enough for a vacation game kit.
