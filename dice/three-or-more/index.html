Rules

Variant status: Ready. Uses a simple race-to-score version with one reroll decision.

Object

Be the first player to reach the target score.

Target scores

  • 20 points = short game
  • 50 points = normal game
  • 100 points = long game

Basic scoring

  • Three of a kind = 3 points
  • Four of a kind = 6 points
  • Five of a kind = 12 points

How a turn works

  1. Roll 5 dice.
  2. If you roll three-of-a-kind or better, score it immediately.
  3. If you roll exactly one pair, choose either to keep the pair and reroll the other 3 dice, or reroll all 5 dice.
  4. If the reroll creates three-or-more of a kind, score it.
  5. If you roll no pair and no set, score 0.

Final-turn rule

Once a player reaches the target score, every other player gets one final turn. Highest score wins.

Why it works

Three or More gives some of the reroll tension of Yahtzee without a full score sheet of categories.
