Rules

Object

Score as many points as possible in 10 turns, or try to beat a target score.

Setup

Use the normal Farkle scoring chart. Give yourself exactly 10 turns.

How to play

  1. Play each turn like Farkle: roll 6 dice, set aside at least one scoring die or combo, then bank or keep rolling.
  2. If you roll no scoring dice, you Farkle and score 0 for that turn.
  3. If all 6 dice score, you may roll all 6 again and keep building the same turn score.

Targets

  • 3,000 = decent run
  • 5,000 = strong game
  • 7,500+ = excellent solo score
