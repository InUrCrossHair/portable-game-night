Rules draft

Object

Be the first player to reach a target score, often 10,000 points. For a shorter game, use 5,000 points.

Basic scoring

Official scoring uses Cody’s Farkle score sheet:

  • Single 1 = 100 points
  • Single 5 = 50 points
  • Three 1s = 1,000 points
  • Three 2s = 200 points
  • Three 3s = 300 points
  • Three 4s = 400 points
  • Three 5s = 500 points
  • Three 6s = 600 points
  • Four of a kind = 2x the triplet score
  • Five of a kind = 3x the triplet score
  • Six of a kind = 4x the triplet score
  • Straight, 1-2-3-4-5-6 = 1,500 points
  • Three pairs = 1,500 points
  • Four of a kind with a pair = 1,500 points
  • Two triplets = 2,500 points

How a turn works

  1. Roll all 6 dice.
  2. Set aside at least one scoring die or scoring combination.
  3. Decide whether to stop and bank your points, or keep rolling the remaining dice.
  4. If all 6 dice score, you get “hot dice” and may roll all 6 again to keep adding points.
  5. If you ever roll and get no scoring dice, that is a Farkle. You lose all unbanked points from that turn, and your turn ends.

Why it works

Farkle is easy to learn because the decision is always simple: take the safe points now, or risk them for more. The game gets loud when someone pushes too far and loses a big turn.
