Rules

Object

Fill five scoring categories in five rounds for the highest total.

Setup

Use 5 dice. Write five categories: Ones, Pairs, Three-of-a-kind, Straight, Chance.

How to play

  1. Each round, roll up to three times, keeping any dice between rolls.
  2. After your final roll, choose one unused category and score it. Each category can be used once.
  3. Ones: score 10 points per 1. Pairs: score total of one pair. Three-of-a-kind: score total of all dice if you have at least three matching. Straight: 30 points for 1-2-3-4-5 or 2-3-4-5-6. Chance: score total of all dice.

Win

Try to beat your personal best. 75+ is solid; 100+ is excellent.

First-time player clarifications

  • This is a solo puzzle. Set a clear target before starting, such as highest score after 10 turns.
  • Write down each turn score immediately so you do not have to remember prior rolls.
  • If a choice is ambiguous, use the same choice consistently for the whole game rather than changing mid-game.
