Rules

Object

Be the first player to reach 500 points by clearing numbered spaces from a fresh board each turn. The board has numbers 2 through 12. Harder totals are worth more points.

Board scoring

  • 2 = 50 points
  • 3 = 25 points
  • 4 = 17 points
  • 5 = 13 points
  • 6 = 10 points
  • 7 = 5 points
  • 8 = 10 points
  • 9 = 13 points
  • 10 = 17 points
  • 11 = 25 points
  • 12 = 50 points

Recommended board-clear bonus: 100 points if you clear all numbers 2–12 in a single turn.

Setup

Each player starts at 0 points. On every turn, the active player starts with a fresh board showing numbers 2–12. Use poker chips/tokens to cover numbers, or mark them on the score sheet.

How a turn works

  1. Roll all 6 dice.
  2. Pair two unused dice together to make a total from 2–12.
  3. If that number is still open on your board, clear it and add its points to your turn score.
  4. Those two dice are now used and cannot be used again until you roll again.
  5. Keep making valid pairs from the dice available.
  6. If you use all 6 dice successfully, roll all 6 dice again and keep building the same turn score.
  7. If you have unused dice left, you may stop and bank your turn score, or roll only the remaining unused dice.
  8. If you roll and cannot make any valid pair that clears an open number, you bust and lose all unbanked points from that turn.

Example

You roll 2, 2, 4, 3, 1, 5.

A strong play could be:

  • 1 + 2 = 3, clear 3 for 25 points
  • 5 + 4 = 9, clear 9 for 13 points
  • 2 + 3 = 5, clear 5 for 13 points

That uses all 6 dice, scores 51 points, and lets you reroll all 6 dice.

Strategy

The rare totals are the dangerous ones. 2 and 12 require exactly 1+1 or 6+6, so they are worth 50 points each and are often worth clearing early. 7 is the easiest total because it can be made with 1+6, 2+5, or 3+4, so it is only worth 5 points. Bad pairing choices can paint you into a corner even when the roll looked good at first.

Why it works

Pair Pressure feels like Shut the Box crossed with a push-your-luck race. The arithmetic is simple, but every pair choice matters because each die can only be used once before the next roll.

First-time player clarifications

  • Roll 6 dice and pair dice to cover target totals on the 2–12 board. Each die can be used only once before rerolling.
  • If you use all 6 dice, pick them all back up and keep going with a fresh roll.
  • If only 1 unused die remains, you cannot make another pair; bank if you can, or continue only if the rules tell you to reroll after using all dice.
  • If no legal pair can cover an open target, you bust and lose the unbanked turn score. First to the target score wins.
