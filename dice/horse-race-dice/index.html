Rules

Object

Cheer for numbered horses as dice rolls move them toward the finish.

Setup

  • Use horses numbered 2 through 12 on a race track.
  • Each player chooses or bets tokens on one or more horses.

How to play

  1. Roll 2 dice. Move the horse matching the total one space forward.
  2. Keep rolling around the table until one horse reaches the finish.
  3. Players who picked or bet on the winning horse win the round.

Recommended track lengths

Use probability-balanced lengths: 2 and 12 need 3 spaces; 3 and 11 need 4; 4 and 10 need 5; 5 and 9 need 6; 6 and 8 need 7; 7 needs 8.

Party note

This is mostly cheering, groaning, and blaming the dice. Which is to say: useful science.
