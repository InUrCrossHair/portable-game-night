Rules

Object

Guess whether the total of two dice will be odd or even.

Setup

Use 2 dice and a cup. Players may use candy, coins, chips, or tokens if they want a scorekeeping pot.

How to play

  1. The roller shakes the dice under a cup.
  2. Each player calls Cho/even or Han/odd.
  3. Reveal the dice. Correct callers win the round or take one token from the pot.

Party variant

Let players place one token on odd or even before the reveal. Correct bets win even money from a shared bank.
