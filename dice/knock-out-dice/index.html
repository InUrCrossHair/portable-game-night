Rules

Object

Score points while avoiding your personal knockout number.

Setup

  • Each player chooses a knockout number from 6, 7, 8, or 9. For a simpler game, everyone uses 7.
  • Choose a target score such as 50.

How to play

  1. On your turn, roll 2 dice and add the total.
  2. If you roll your knockout number, your turn ends and you score 0 for that turn.
  3. Otherwise, add the roll to your turn score and roll again or bank.

Win

First player to the target score wins. For elimination, lose a life whenever you roll your knockout number.
