Rules

Object

Use the Pair Pressure board to score as many points as possible in 10 turns.

Setup

Use the normal Pair Pressure scoring board from 2 through 12. The board resets at the start of every turn.

How to play

  1. Roll 6 dice and pair dice to clear numbers 2–12. Each die can only be used once until you reroll.
  2. Score each cleared number. If all 6 dice are used successfully, reroll all 6 and continue the same turn.
  3. If you reroll and cannot make a playable pair, you bust and score 0 for that turn.
  4. Bank whenever you want before rerolling. A full board clear in one turn adds the normal board-clear bonus.

Solo scoring

Play 10 turns. Track your best total, best single turn, and number of full-board clears.

First-time player clarifications

  • Use the normal Pair Pressure board and scoring chart.
  • Play a fixed number of turns, usually 10, and try to beat your previous score.
  • When no legal pair can cover an open number, the turn busts unless you banked before rolling.
  • Keep the scoring chart visible; the solo challenge is much easier to learn when the board and values are in front of you.
