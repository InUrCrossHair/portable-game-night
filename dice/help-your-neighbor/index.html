Rules draft

Variant status: Ready. Uses a numbered-player token removal version.

Object

Be the first player to get rid of all tokens.

Setup

  1. Give each player 10 tokens.
  2. Assign each player a number:
  • With 3 players: use 1, 2, 3
  • With 4 players: use 1, 2, 3, 4
  • With 5 players: use 1, 2, 3, 4, 5
  • With 6 players: use 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6

How a turn works

  1. Roll 3 dice.
  2. For each die that matches another player’s number, that player removes 1 token.
  3. For each die that matches your own number, you remove 1 token.
  4. For unused numbers, nothing happens.
  5. First player to remove all tokens wins.

Example

Four players are numbered 1–4.

You roll:


1, 1, 4

Player 1 removes 2 tokens. Player 4 removes 1 token.

Why it works

It is easy, works with 3–6 people, and turns the dice into a silly “who gets helped?” engine. It is not strategic, but it fits the travel-kit filler category.
