Object

Reach the chosen target score first.

Recommended target scores:

  • 50 points = short game
  • 100 points = normal game
  • 200 points = long game

Recommended final-turn rule: once a player reaches the target score, every other player gets one final turn to try to beat or tie the leader. Highest score after those final turns wins.

How a turn works

  1. Roll 5 dice.
  2. Any 2s or 5s are stuck in the mud. Set them aside and score 0 for those dice.
  3. Add the remaining dice to your turn score.
  4. Reroll only the dice that are not stuck.
  5. Continue until all dice are stuck.
  6. Add the turn score to your total.

Why it works

It is easy to explain and has a fun visual feel as your available dice slowly disappear. It is mostly luck, but good for low-pressure play.
