Object

Have the lowest total score after 9 or 18 holes.

How it could work

Dice Golf has many rule sets. A clean house version should define:

  • How many dice are rolled per hole
  • How many rerolls are allowed
  • What counts as a good or bad hole score
  • Whether special combinations affect score

Why it works

The golf theme is easy to understand, and a printable scorecard could make it feel more polished. This is a lower-priority add until the house rules are finalized.
