Rules draft

Variant status: Ready. Uses a clean 9-hole scorecard version with simple rerolls.

Object

Have the lowest score after 9 holes.

Game length

  • 9 holes = normal game
  • 18 holes = long game; play the 9-hole card twice

How a hole works

  1. Roll 5 dice.
  2. Choose any dice to keep.
  3. Reroll the rest.
  4. You may roll up to 3 times total.
  5. Score the final roll using the chart below.

Scoring chart

Lower is better:

  • Five of a kind = 1 stroke, hole-in-one
  • Four of a kind = 2 strokes
  • Full house = 3 strokes
  • Straight, 1-2-3-4-5 or 2-3-4-5-6 = 4 strokes
  • Three of a kind = 5 strokes
  • Two pair = 6 strokes
  • One pair = 7 strokes
  • No combination = 8 strokes

Optional hazard rule

If your final roll has no pair and totals 20 or higher, add +1 stroke. Skip this rule for the first few games.

Why it works

The golf theme is easy to understand, and the scoring chart gives it a satisfying golf feel without needing a custom hole-by-hole rulebook.
