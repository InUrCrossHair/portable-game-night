Rules

Object

Be the first player to reach a target score, often 100.

How a turn works

  1. Roll two dice.
  2. If the total is not 7, add the total to your temporary turn score.
  3. Choose whether to roll again or stop and bank your points.
  4. If you roll a 7, your turn ends and you lose the unbanked points from that turn.

Why it works

Sevens Out is basically a two-dice cousin of Pig. The danger number is common enough that pushing your luck feels genuinely risky.
