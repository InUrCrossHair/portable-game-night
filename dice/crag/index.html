Rules

Variant status: Ready. Uses a compact 13-category scorecard version.

Object

Have the highest score after all categories are filled.

How a turn works

  1. Roll 3 dice.
  2. Choose any dice to keep.
  3. Reroll the rest.
  4. You may roll up to 3 times total.
  5. Score the result in one open category.

Recommended scorecard

Upper section:

  • Ones = total of 1s
  • Twos = total of 2s
  • Threes = total of 3s
  • Fours = total of 4s
  • Fives = total of 5s
  • Sixes = total of 6s

Lower section:

  • Pair = total of all dice if at least two match
  • Three of a Kind = total of all dice if all three match
  • Low Straight = 1-2-3 = 20 points
  • High Straight = 4-5-6 = 20 points
  • Thirteen = any dice totaling 13 = 26 points
  • Crag = a pair plus a total of 13 = 50 points
  • Chance = total of all dice

Bonus

No upper-section bonus. Keep it simple.

Why it works

Crag feels like Yahtzee but is much shorter. The 50-point Crag gives the game a memorable jackpot moment.

First-time player clarifications

  • Each turn, roll 3 dice up to three times, keeping any dice between rolls.
  • After rolling, choose one empty scorecard category. You must fill one category every turn.
  • Use the printable scorecard so players can see categories and avoid arguing over scoring.
  • When all categories are filled, highest total wins.
