Rules draft

Variant status: House-rule version needed. Recommended direction: choose one clean scorecard before making printables.

Object

Score the most points after all scorecard categories are filled.

Common categories

Crag rules vary, but common categories include:

  • Ones through sixes
  • Pair
  • Three of a kind
  • Straight
  • Thirteen
  • Crag, often a pair plus a total of 13

Why it works

Crag is useful when Yahtzee feels too long but you still want a real scorecard game with choices.
