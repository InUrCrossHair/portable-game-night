Rules draft

Object

Score the most points after each player completes a turn, or after a set number of rounds.

How a turn works

  1. Roll 5 dice.
  2. Any dice showing 2 or 5 are dead. Remove them.
  3. Add the remaining dice to your score.
  4. Roll the remaining live dice again.
  5. Continue until all dice are dead.
  6. Your total for that turn is your score.

Example

You roll 6, 4, 5, 3, and 2.

  • The 5 and 2 are dead and removed.
  • Score the remaining dice: 6 + 4 + 3 = 13.
  • Roll the remaining 3 dice.

Why it works

Drop Dead is simple, but it has a good survival feeling. Players cheer for big scoring rolls and groan when half their dice die immediately.
