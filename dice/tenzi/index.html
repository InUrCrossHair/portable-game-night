Rules

Object

Be the first player to get all 10 of your dice showing the same number.

Setup

Give every player 10 dice. Each player chooses their own rolling area.

How a round works

  1. Everyone rolls all 10 dice at the same time.
  2. After the first roll, choose a number to chase. For example, if you rolled several 4s, you might choose 4.
  3. Set aside dice showing your chosen number.
  4. Reroll the rest as quickly as possible.
  5. Keep setting aside matching dice and rerolling non-matching dice.
  6. The first player with all 10 dice matching shouts “Tenzi” and wins the round.

Why it works

Tenzi is less about strategy and more about energy. It is good for groups that want speed and laughter instead of careful turns.
