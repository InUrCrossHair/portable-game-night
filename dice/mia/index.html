Rules draft

Object

Avoid losing rounds. Many groups give players lives, tokens, or points. The last player with lives remaining wins.

How rolls are read

Rolls are usually read with the higher die first.

Examples:

  • 6 and 5 = 65
  • 4 and 2 = 42
  • 3 and 1 = 31

Common ranking

From highest to lower:

  1. 21, called Mia, is usually the highest roll.
  2. Doubles, such as 66, 55, 44, and so on.
  3. Other two-digit rolls, highest number first, such as 65, 64, 63, down to 31.

How a turn works

  1. A player rolls two dice secretly under a cup.
  2. They announce a result, truthfully or not.
  3. The next player chooses either to believe them or call them a liar.
  4. If the next player believes them, they take the cup, roll secretly, and must announce a higher result.
  5. If someone calls liar, the dice are revealed.
  6. If the announced result was false, the liar loses the round. If it was true, the caller loses the round.

Why it works

Mia is basically tiny Liar’s Dice. It is fast, suspicious, and funny because the pressure builds as announced rolls get higher.
