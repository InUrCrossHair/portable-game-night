Rules

Variant status: Ready. Uses a clean 3-lives bluffing version.

Object

Be the last player with lives remaining.

Setup

  1. Each player starts with 3 lives.
  2. Use 2 dice and one cup.
  3. Players take turns rolling secretly under the cup.

How rolls are read

Rolls are read as a two-digit number with the higher die first.

Examples:

  • 6 and 5 = 65
  • 4 and 2 = 42
  • 3 and 1 = 31

Roll ranking

From highest to lowest:

  1. 21, called Mia, is the highest possible roll.
  2. Doubles, highest to lowest: 66, 55, 44, 33, 22, 11.
  3. Normal rolls, highest to lowest: 65 down to 31.

How a turn works

  1. A player rolls two dice secretly under a cup.
  2. They announce a result, truthfully or not.
  3. The next player chooses either to believe them or challenge.
  4. If the next player believes them, they take the cup, roll secretly, and must announce a higher result.
  5. If someone challenges, the dice are revealed.
  6. If the announced result was false, the roller loses 1 life.
  7. If the announced result was true, the challenger loses 1 life.
  8. After a challenge, start a new round.

Mia / 21 rule

If someone announces Mia, the next player may still challenge. There is no extra special penalty; Mia is simply the highest possible announced result.

Why it works

Mia is basically tiny Liar’s Dice. It is fast, suspicious, and funny because the pressure builds as announced rolls get higher.

First-time player clarifications

  • Read the two dice as a two-digit number with the higher die first. A 6 and 3 is 63, not 9.
  • The usual high roll is 21, called Mia. Then doubles rank high, then normal numbers from 65 down to 31.
  • On your turn, you may tell the truth or bluff about your roll, then pass the covered dice to the next player.
  • The next player either accepts and must announce a higher result, or challenges. The loser of the challenge loses a life.
