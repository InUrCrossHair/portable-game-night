Rules

Object

Be the first player to reach a target score, often 100 points. For a shorter game, use 50.

One-die version

On your turn:

  1. Roll one die.
  2. If you roll 2–6, add that number to your temporary turn total.
  3. You may stop and bank your turn total at any time.
  4. If you roll a 1, you lose all unbanked points from that turn, and your turn ends.

Two-dice version

On your turn:

  1. Roll two dice.
  2. If neither die is a 1, add both dice to your temporary turn total.
  3. You may stop and bank your turn total at any time.
  4. If one die is a 1, you lose the points from that turn.
  5. If both dice are 1s, many versions make you lose your entire game score, not just the turn score.

Why it works

Pig is the simplest possible “do I stop or keep going?” game. It is not deep, but it is excellent for kids, casual players, or quick filler rounds.

First-time player clarifications

  • Default beginner rule: use 1 die. Roll as many times as you want, adding the roll to your turn total.
  • If you roll a 1, your turn ends and you score 0 for that turn only.
  • If you stop before rolling a 1, bank your turn total into your game score.
  • First to 100 wins. For two-dice Pig, use the same idea: rolling any 1 ends your turn and loses only the current turn total.
