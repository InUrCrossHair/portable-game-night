Object

Be the last player with tokens remaining, or win the center pot depending on the house rules.

How a turn works

  1. Give each player 2 or 3 tokens.
  2. On your turn, roll 2 dice.
  3. For each 1 rolled, put one token into the center pot.
  4. For each 6 rolled, pass one token to the player on your left.
  5. Other numbers do nothing.
  6. Players with no tokens skip rolling, but can reenter if someone passes them a token.

Why it works

Aces in the Pot is mostly luck, but it is easy, social, and works well when people are talking or snacking.
