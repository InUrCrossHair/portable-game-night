Rules

Variant status: Ready. Uses a simple non-gambling token elimination version.

Object

Be the last player with tokens.

Setup

Each player starts with 3 tokens.

How a turn works

On your turn, roll 2 dice.

For each die:

  • 1 = put 1 token into the center pot
  • 6 = pass 1 token to the player on your left
  • 2, 3, 4, 5 = no effect

If you roll two effect dice, resolve both.

If you have fewer tokens than effects

Resolve as many effects as you can. You cannot pass or pay tokens you do not have.

If you have no tokens

You skip your turn, but you are not out permanently. If someone passes you a token, you re-enter.

End of game

When only one player has tokens, that player wins.

Why it works

Aces in the Pot is mostly luck, but it keeps the classic chip-passing feel without needing money or complicated elimination rules.
