Rules

Object

Be the first player to complete a beetle drawing.

Common body-part rules

  • 6 = Body
  • 5 = Head
  • 4 = Wing
  • 3 = Leg
  • 2 = Antenna
  • 1 = Eye

A common completed beetle needs:

  • 1 body
  • 1 head
  • 2 wings
  • 6 legs
  • 2 antennae
  • 2 eyes

How a turn works

  1. On your turn, roll one die.
  2. If the number matches a body part you can add, draw that part.
  3. If you cannot use that result, your turn ends with no progress.
  4. Pass the die to the next player.

Important restriction

Most versions require the body before anything else, and the head before eyes or antennae.

Why it works

Beetle is more cute than strategic. It is useful when younger kids are playing or when you want a no-pressure game.

First-time player clarifications

  • Each player needs paper and starts with a blank beetle drawing.
  • You usually must roll the body before adding other parts, because every other part attaches to it.
  • If you roll a part you cannot draw yet or already completed, nothing happens and the turn passes.
  • First player to complete their beetle wins.
