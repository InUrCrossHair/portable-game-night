Rules

Object

Score the most points after all target rounds are complete.

How a game works

  1. Round 1 target is 2.
  2. Round 2 target is 3.
  3. Continue until the final round target is 12.
  4. On each round, every player rolls two dice once.
  5. If your dice total equals the target number, you score that number.
  6. If your dice do not equal the target, you score 0 for that round.

Example

The target is 8. You roll 5 and 3. Your total is 8, so you score 8 points.

Why it works

Chicago is mostly luck, but it moves fast and works well as a warm-up game or tiebreaker.

First-time player clarifications

  • Play 11 rounds with target totals from 2 through 12.
  • In each round, every player rolls 2 dice once. If your total matches the round target, score that target number; otherwise score 0.
  • After round 12, highest total score wins. Ties can be broken with one extra roll each.
