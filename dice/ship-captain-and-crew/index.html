Rules draft

Object

Have the highest cargo score after everyone gets a turn.

What the dice mean

  • 6 = Ship
  • 5 = Captain
  • 4 = Crew
  • The remaining two dice = Cargo

You cannot score cargo unless you have a ship, captain, and crew.

How a turn works

  1. Roll 5 dice.
  2. You need to keep a 6 first. This is your ship.
  3. After you have a 6, you need a 5. This is your captain.
  4. After you have a 5, you need a 4. This is your crew.
  5. Once you have 6-5-4, the two leftover dice are added together as your cargo score.
  6. Most versions give each player up to 3 rolls total.

Example

You roll: 6, 5, 3, 2, 1.

  • Keep the 6 as your ship.
  • Keep the 5 as your captain.
  • You still need a 4 for crew.
  • Reroll the other three dice.

If you later roll a 4, you keep it. Then your two remaining dice become cargo.

Why it works

It is simple, fast, and has just enough suspense. You might roll a ship and captain quickly but fail to get crew, or you might qualify and end up with terrible cargo.
