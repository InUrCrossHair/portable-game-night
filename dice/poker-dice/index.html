Rules

Object

Make a better dice hand than the other players.

How a turn works

  1. Roll 5 dice.
  2. Choose any dice to keep.
  3. Reroll the rest.
  4. Choose again.
  5. Reroll one final time if desired.
  6. Compare your final hand to the other players’ hands.

Common hand ranking

From strongest to weakest:

  1. Five of a kind
  2. Four of a kind
  3. Full house
  4. Straight
  5. Three of a kind
  6. Two pair
  7. One pair
  8. High die

Why it works

Poker Dice gives the satisfying part of Yahtzee without the bookkeeping. It is easy to play several short rounds in a row.
