Rules

Object

Play a baseball game with dice. Score the most runs after 9 innings.

Setup

  • Use 2 dice. Each player or team gets 9 batting innings.
  • Use tokens, coins, or pencil marks for runners on first, second, and third.

How to play

  1. Roll 2 dice for each at-bat and apply the result table.
  2. Three outs ends the half-inning. Clear the bases when the inning ends.
  3. Advance runners the number of bases shown by singles, doubles, triples, and home runs.

Recommended result table

  • 2 = home run
  • 3 = triple
  • 4 = double
  • 5 or 6 = single
  • 7, 8, or 9 = out
  • 10 = walk
  • 11 = strikeout / out
  • 12 = double play if runner on base, otherwise out

Team play

For a party, split into two teams and rotate who rolls each at-bat.

First-time player clarifications

  • Play 9 innings. Each half-inning ends after 3 outs.
  • Use the result chart to decide whether a roll is an out, walk, single, double, triple, or home run.
  • On hits, runners advance the matching number of bases. On a walk, only forced runners advance.
  • If tied after 9 innings, play extra innings until one player leads after a complete inning.
