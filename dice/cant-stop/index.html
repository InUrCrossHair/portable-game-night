Rules draft

Object

Be the first player to claim three number columns.

Setup

Create columns numbered 2 through 12. The middle numbers, especially 6, 7, and 8, should have longer tracks because they are easier to roll. The edge numbers, like 2 and 12, should have shorter tracks because they are harder to roll.

How a turn works

  1. Roll 4 dice.
  2. Split the 4 dice into two pairs.
  3. Each pair creates a sum.
  4. Move temporary markers up those number columns.
  5. Decide whether to stop and save progress, or roll again.
  6. If you roll and cannot move in one of your active columns, you bust and lose the temporary progress from that turn.

Example

You roll 2, 3, 5, and 6.

Possible pairings include:

  • 2 + 3 = 5 and 5 + 6 = 11
  • 2 + 5 = 7 and 3 + 6 = 9
  • 2 + 6 = 8 and 3 + 5 = 8

You choose the pairing that helps your board position.

Why it works

Can’t Stop is basically Farkle with a better strategic board. You constantly think, “I can probably roll one more time,” and that is exactly when the dice betray you.
