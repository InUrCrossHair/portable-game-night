Rules

Object

Create a higher number than the other players.

How a round works

  1. Decide how many dice to use.
  2. Each player rolls that many dice.
  3. Arrange your dice into the largest possible number.
  4. Highest number wins the round.

Examples

With two dice:

  • Roll 3 and 6.
  • Best number is 63, not 36.

With three dice:

  • Roll 2, 5, and 6.
  • Best number is 652.

Why it works

Beat That is extremely easy and works well with younger players. It is not deep, but it gives fast little competitive moments.
