Rules draft

Variant status: Ready. Uses a 3-roll version where players must keep at least one die per roll.

Object

Score the highest total in the round.

Required dice

To score, a player must have:

  • One 1
  • One 4

These required dice are set aside and do not count toward the score.

How a turn works

  1. Roll 6 dice.
  2. After each roll, keep at least one die.
  3. You may keep any dice, but to score you must eventually keep a 1 and a 4.
  4. You get up to 3 rolls total.
  5. After your final roll, if you have a 1 and 4, add the other four dice.
  6. If you do not have both a 1 and 4, your score is 0.

Example

Final dice:


1, 4, 6, 6, 5, 2

Score:


6 + 6 + 5 + 2 = 19

Winning

Highest score wins the round.

Tie rule

Tied players reroll one full turn.

Optional game length

  • Quick game: best of 3 rounds
  • Normal game: first to 5 round wins
  • Longer game: play 10 rounds and total scores

Why it works

Midnight feels close to Ship, Captain, and Crew, but it is easier to qualify and usually gives bigger, more flexible scores. The “must keep at least one die” rule prevents endless fishing for perfect rolls.
