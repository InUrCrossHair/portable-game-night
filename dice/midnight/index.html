Rules draft

Variant status: House-rule version needed. Recommended direction: each player gets up to 3 rolls and must keep at least one die per roll.

Object

Have the highest score after everyone takes a turn.

How a turn works

  1. Roll 6 dice.
  2. You must set aside a 1 and a 4 before you can score.
  3. After each roll, keep at least one die.
  4. You may roll up to 3 times total.
  5. If you end with a 1 and 4, add the other four dice together as your score.
  6. If you never get both the 1 and 4, your score is 0 for the round.

Why it works

Midnight feels close to Ship, Captain, and Crew, but it is easier to qualify and usually gives bigger, more flexible scores.
