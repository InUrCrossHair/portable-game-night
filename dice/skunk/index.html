Rules draft

Variant status: House-rule version needed. Recommended direction: classic S-K-U-N-K five-round structure.

Object

Score the most points after five rounds, one round for each letter in S-K-U-N-K.

How a round works

  1. Everyone starts the round active.
  2. Before each roll, active players may choose to stay in or sit out.
  3. Roll two dice.
  4. If no 1 appears, active players add the dice total to their temporary round score.
  5. If one 1 appears, active players lose their temporary points for that round and the round ends.
  6. If double 1s appear, active players lose all game points, not just the round points.
  7. Players who sat out keep the points they had already earned that round.

Why it works

Skunk is like Pig, but better for groups because everyone is making risk decisions at the same time. It creates good table moments without needing complicated scoring.
