Rules

Variant status: Ready. Uses classic S-K-U-N-K with a fair tie rule.

Object

Have the highest score after five rounds.

Rounds

The game has five rounds:


S K U N K

Each letter is one round.

How a round works

  1. Everyone starts the round active.
  2. Before each roll, each active player chooses to stay in or sit out.
  3. Players who sit out keep their current points for the round.
  4. Roll 2 dice.
  5. If no 1 is rolled, active players add the dice total to their temporary round score.
  6. If one 1 is rolled, all active players lose their temporary points for that round. The round ends.
  7. If double 1s are rolled, all active players lose all points for the whole game. The round ends.
  8. Players who already sat out keep the points they banked that round.

End of game

After the final K round, highest total score wins.

Tie rule

If tied, play one sudden-death extra round. Highest score from that round wins.

Why it works

Skunk is like Pig, but better for groups because everyone is making risk decisions at the same time. The double-1 penalty is harsh, but that is what gives the game its bite.

First-time player clarifications

  • Everyone stands in for a round if they want to keep risking points. Players may sit down before a roll to bank points for that round.
  • If a 1 appears, standing players lose the unbanked points for that round. If double 1s appear, use your chosen house penalty before starting.
  • Play one round for each letter in SKUNK. Highest total after all 5 rounds wins.
