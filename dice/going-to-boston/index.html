Rules draft

Object

Score the highest total for the round.

How a turn works

  1. Roll 3 dice.
  2. Keep the highest die.
  3. Roll the remaining 2 dice.
  4. Keep the highest of those.
  5. Roll the final die.
  6. Add the three kept dice together. That is your score for the round.

Example

First roll: 2, 5, 6. Keep 6. Second roll with two dice: 1, 4. Keep 4. Final roll: 3. Your total is 6 + 4 + 3 = 13.

Winning

You can play one round at a time, or play multiple rounds and keep a running total. First to 100 is a simple target.

Why it works

There is almost no learning curve. It is mostly luck, but it gives everyone a quick turn and works well when you want something lighter than Farkle.
