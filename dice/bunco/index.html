Rules

Object

Score points by rolling dice that match the round number. For example, in round 3, you want to roll 3s.

Setup

Bunco is often played with multiple tables of four players, but you can simplify it for smaller groups.

For a simple version:

  1. Use 3 dice.
  2. Choose a round number from 1 to 6.
  3. Players take turns rolling.
  4. Score based on how many dice match the round number.

Basic scoring

Common scoring:

  • One matching die = 1 point
  • Two matching dice = 2 points
  • Three matching dice = 5 points
  • Three matching dice that match the round number = Bunco, often worth 21 points

Example: In round 4, rolling 4-4-4 is Bunco.

How a turn works

  1. Roll all 3 dice.
  2. Score any matching dice.
  3. If you scored at least one point, roll again.
  4. If you score zero, your turn ends.

Why it works

Bunco is mostly luck, but it shines as a social game. It is better for a group event than a strategic game night.
