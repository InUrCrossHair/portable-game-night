Rules

Object

Roll the best five-dice hand after up to three rolls.

Setup

Choose a starting player. Use 5 dice and optional tokens for round wins.

How to play

  1. On your turn, roll up to three times, keeping any dice you like between rolls.
  2. After your final roll, record or announce your hand.
  3. Next player tries to beat it. Best hand wins the round.

Recommended rankings

  • Five of a kind
  • Four of a kind
  • Full house
  • Straight: 1-2-3-4-5 or 2-3-4-5-6
  • Three of a kind
  • Two pair
  • One pair
  • High die

First-time player clarifications

  • Each player gets up to three rolls to make the best hand.
  • Decide before play whether ties use highest dice/kickers or a reroll. For beginners, reroll exact ties.
  • Play 5 rounds. Highest hand wins each round; most round wins wins the game.
