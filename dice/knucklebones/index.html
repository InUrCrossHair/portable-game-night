Rules draft

Object

Have the highest score when one player fills their 3-by-3 grid.

Setup

Each player draws a 3-column by 3-row grid:

[ ] [ ] [ ]

Each column can hold up to 3 dice.

How a turn works

  1. Roll one die.
  2. Place it in any open space in one of your three columns.
  3. If your opponent has dice with the same number in the matching column on their board, remove those dice from their board.
  4. Players alternate turns until one player fills all 9 spaces.
  5. Both players total their scores.

Scoring

Each column is scored separately.

  • A single die scores its face value.
  • Matching dice in the same column multiply each other.

Common scoring examples:

  • One 5 in a column = 5 points
  • Two 5s in the same column = 5 + 5, then multiplied by 2 = 20 points
  • Three 5s in the same column = 5 + 5 + 5, then multiplied by 3 = 45 points

Why it works

Knucklebones is one of the best repeat-play dice games because it is not just random rolling. Every placement matters. You can build your own score, attack your opponent’s column, block future damage, or gamble on stacking matching dice.
