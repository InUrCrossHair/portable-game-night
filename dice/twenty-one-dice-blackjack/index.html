Rules

Object

Get closer to 21 than the other players without going over.

Setup

Choose a dealer or just play round-by-round with everyone rolling for themselves.

How to play

  1. On your turn, roll 2 dice and add the total.
  2. After each roll, stop or roll again.
  3. If your total goes over 21, you bust and score 0 for the round.
  4. After everyone stops or busts, closest to 21 wins the round. Ties share the win or reroll.

Optional scoring

Score 1 point per round win. First to 5 wins, or use tokens/candy for round markers.

First-time player clarifications

  • For a simple game, do not use a dealer. Each player takes one turn trying to get close to 21 without going over.
  • Roll 2 dice, add the total, then choose to roll again or stop.
  • If you go over 21, you bust and score 0 for the round.
  • After everyone has one turn, closest to 21 wins the round. Ties share the win or reroll.
