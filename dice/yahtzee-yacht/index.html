Rules

Object

Score the most points after everyone fills all scoring categories.

How a turn works

  1. Roll 5 dice.
  2. Choose any dice to keep.
  3. Reroll the rest.
  4. Choose dice to keep again.
  5. Reroll one final time if desired.
  6. After up to 3 rolls total, choose one open category on your score sheet and write your score there.

Common categories

Upper section:

  • Ones
  • Twos
  • Threes
  • Fours
  • Fives
  • Sixes

For these, you score only dice matching that number. For example, if you choose Fives and rolled 5, 5, 5, 2, 1, you score 15.

Lower section:

  • Three of a kind
  • Four of a kind
  • Full house
  • Small straight
  • Large straight
  • Yahtzee/Yacht, which is five of a kind
  • Chance, which is any roll scored as the total of all dice

Why it works

Yahtzee has more structure than most dice games. Each turn is simple, but the strategy comes from deciding where to place a roll when it does not fit perfectly.
