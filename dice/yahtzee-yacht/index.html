Rules

Object

Score the most points after everyone fills all scoring categories.

How a turn works

  1. Roll 5 dice.
  2. Choose any dice to keep.
  3. Reroll the rest.
  4. Choose dice to keep again.
  5. Reroll one final time if desired.
  6. After up to 3 rolls total, choose one open category on your score sheet and write your score there.

Common categories

Upper section:

  • Ones
  • Twos
  • Threes
  • Fours
  • Fives
  • Sixes

For these, you score only dice matching that number. For example, if you choose Fives and rolled 5, 5, 5, 2, 1, you score 15.

Lower section:

  • Three of a kind
  • Four of a kind
  • Full house
  • Small straight
  • Large straight
  • Yahtzee/Yacht, which is five of a kind
  • Chance, which is any roll scored as the total of all dice

Why it works

Yahtzee has more structure than most dice games. Each turn is simple, but the strategy comes from deciding where to place a roll when it does not fit perfectly.

First-time player clarifications

  • Each player takes 13 turns. On your turn, roll up to three times, keeping any dice between rolls.
  • After the final roll, choose one empty score category. You must fill one category every turn, even if it scores 0.
  • Upper section: Ones through Sixes score the total of that face only. Example: three 4s score 12 in Fours.
  • Common lower scores: three/four of a kind = total of all dice; full house = 25; small straight = 30; large straight = 40; chance = total of all dice; Yahtzee/Yacht = five of a kind, usually 50.
  • Highest total after all categories are filled wins. Use the printable score sheet so players do not have to remember the categories.
