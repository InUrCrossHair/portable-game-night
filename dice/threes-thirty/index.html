Rules draft

Object

Have the lowest score after everyone takes a turn.

How a turn works

  1. Roll 5 dice.
  2. You may keep any dice and reroll the others.
  3. You get up to 3 rolls total, like Yahtzee.
  4. When you stop, add your dice.
  5. Dice showing 3 count as 0. All other dice count normally.

Example

You finish with 3, 3, 1, 5, and 6.

Your score is 0 + 0 + 1 + 5 + 6 = 12.

Why it works

It flips the usual dice instinct. High rolls are bad, and 3s are suddenly valuable. That makes the reroll decisions feel different from Yahtzee or Poker Dice.
