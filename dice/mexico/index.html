Rules

Object

Avoid having the lowest roll each round, and use bluffing to pressure the next player.

Setup

  • Each player starts with 3 lives/tokens. Use 2 dice and a cup.
  • Roll rankings high to low: 21 is Mexico and highest; doubles next from 66 down to 11; then normal two-digit rolls with high die first, from 65 down to 31.

How to play

  1. First player rolls secretly, announces a score, and passes the cup.
  2. Next player may believe it and roll, needing to announce a higher score, or challenge.
  3. If challenged and the announced score was false, the bluffer loses a life. If it was true, the challenger loses a life.
  4. A player who rolls may tell the truth or bluff before passing.

Win

Players with no lives are out. Last player remaining wins.
